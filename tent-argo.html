Argo Tents

The real explorer's option, named after the famous boat that took Jason and the Argonauts to the Golden Fleece. The smallest of our tents is ideal for guests who prefer the more traditional camping accommodation and keep an active itinerary — discovering the island or enjoying the sea and watersports. The baby Argo offers 24m² living space; the tent interior is 18m² (6m × 3m) and includes a luxurious queen-size bed (or two singles) and basic furnishings, an air cooler and a mini fridge, while the 3m × 2m shaded deck has a rustic table and chairs made from timber logs. Bathrooms and showers are shared.
