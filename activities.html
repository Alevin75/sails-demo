Activities

Curated activities & experiences

Moments that make memories

Each week we host a lineup designed to spark curiosity, strengthen family bonds and deepen your connection to Greece: our open-air cinema with Greek-themed films, mosaic workshops, cooking classes, jewellery making, watermelon-eating contests and camp games.

Back at Sails, unwind with an in-house massage or let us arrange a private yoga session tailored just for you.

Beyond our doors

We have scoured Kos to bring you the island's finest experiences: sunset horseback riding, mountain hiking, water sports and traditional bread-making classes.

On the water

Boat trips to the volcano of Nisyros and the small islands, swimming stops and snorkelling along the coast.

On horseback, on foot

Sunset rides along the beach and walks up to the villages under Mount Dikeos.

Taste and craft

Bread-making classes, wine tasting and the island's markets.

Kids @Sails

A place for families to unwind together

We are parents ourselves, so we know what a young family needs. A shaded children's corner with games, books and toys for babies and toddlers, and a playground where the little ones can let loose — while the adults enjoy a cocktail or a meal in peace.

Toy corner Playground Open-air cinema

Family-run eco-luxury glamping retreat near Marmari Beach, Kos island, Greece.
