Restaurant charges to your room — smooth from the table to check-out

Most of your guests charge meals and drinks to their room and settle at check-out. This is what that flow can look like for the guest, for the kitchen and for the front desk — with the parts that come from your own systems marked as to be agreed.

Sample data Sync with WebHotelier — to be agreed

How the charge travels

01

Guest orders

The guest orders at the table or at the bar. On the tablet the waiter taps the tent number — no paper, no handwriting.

02

Kitchen and bar see it

The ticket appears on the kitchen screen and at the bar, with the tent number and the time. Courses are ticked off as they leave.

03

The charge lands on the room

The amount is posted against the booking, so the guest can see their running tab at any moment. Posting source: your system — to be agreed

04

Check-out in one glance

The front desk opens the booking and sees the room total, the restaurant total and the balance — no receipt hunting, no surprises.

What is needed from your side — honestly

1. Where the charge lives

Today the charge is posted by your restaurant system and settled in your PMS. If those two talk to each other through an interface, we can read the tab and show it. If not, we leave the money side to them.

2. Who taps the order

Either the waiter on a tablet, or the order is taken as it is today and we only show the result. Both are possible; the second one changes nothing in the restaurant.

3. What the guest sees

A running tab on their own page, or nothing at all until check-out. Your call.

This page is a concept for discussion, not a promise. Nothing here touches your data until we agree what your systems can give us — and we would say so plainly if the answer turns out to be no.
